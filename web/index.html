Everyone's story.

Instagram shows what one person posted. MOMENT is the night itself — made by everyone who was there. Start one, put the QR on the table, and their photos land next to yours.

Get MOMENT for iPhone See how

Start a Moment

Pick what's happening — party, trip, dinner, wedding. You get a QR and a link in one tap.

Everyone scans in

People who were there join and add their side. Their photos stay theirs; the Moment belongs to all of you.

Places come alive

Cafés, beaches and venues get a page of what happened there. Owners can claim it and put the QR on the counter.

NOW, for right now

"Anyone up for drinks?" — say what you're up for, see who joins, make it a Moment. Gone in a few hours.

No servers. No sign-up. Nothing collected.

  • There is no MOMENT backend. Your identity is a key that lives on your iPhone; its fingerprint is your MOMENT ID.
  • Your Moments live in your own iCloud and are shared peer-to-peer with the people you invite. We can't read them.
  • Public Moments carry only a venue — never a person's location. Photos are stripped of GPS and device data before they leave the phone.
  • No analytics or crash SDKs. No email, no phone number, no password to leak.

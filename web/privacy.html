Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-19

What we run

Nothing. MOMENT has no servers of its own. The app talks only to Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) using your Apple ID, and to Apple Maps for venue search.

Your identity

On first launch the app creates a cryptographic key in your iPhone's Keychain. Its fingerprint is your MOMENT ID. It never leaves the device except in a backup you make yourself, encrypted with a recovery phrase only you know.

Your Moments

Private and invite-only Moments are stored in your iCloud private database and shared with invitees through iCloud sharing. The developer cannot access them.

Public Moments and venues

If you set a Moment to Public or choose "show me on Nearby", that content and the venue you picked are stored in the app's public iCloud database, visible to all users and technically readable by the developer account. It never includes your device's location — only the venue.

Photos

Photos and videos are compressed and stripped of GPS, device and lens metadata before upload. Only the capture time is kept, for the timeline.

Analytics

None are sent anywhere. Optional usage counters are stored on your phone and off by default.

Your rights

Delete any Moment, contribution or comment you made at any time. Leaving a Moment removes your access. "Delete everything" in Settings removes local data; deleting your iCloud data for MOMENT is done in iOS Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage.
